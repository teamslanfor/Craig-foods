Our story

Steeped in tradition

Craig Foods, trading as Slemish Bakery, has been baking “a wee taste of home” since 1969.

Located in the heart of Antrim, our bakes are steeped in tradition. Firm favourites are the classics: Iced Diamonds, Jam Rolls and Fifteens, the bakes Northern Ireland grew up with.

We use local ingredients wherever possible, and our goods are made in local bakeries like RJ Kerr’s, a sister company in the Craig Foods family.
