Good to know

Trade questions

Do you deliver to my area?

Delivery areas and days depend on your location. Tell us your postcode on the form, or call 028 9447 1950, and we’ll let you know.

Is there a minimum order?

We’ll talk you through ordering and any minimums when we get in touch about your account.

Can I order through my wholesaler?

Our bakes are available through Musgrave, Costcutter, Hendersons, NISA, Russells, Dunnes, Savage & Whitten and Applegreen. Ask your account manager about the Craig Foods range.

Where can I find allergen and shelf-life information?

It’s printed on every pack, and we can send product specifications to trade customers on request.
